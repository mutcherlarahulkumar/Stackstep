# Start with a working agreement

**Estimated effort:** 20 minutes. **Prerequisite:** none. You do not need to be an expert to begin.

## Why this task matters
A team cannot help with goals it cannot see. Your first artifact is a useful introduction, not a performance of confidence. Throughout this internship, ship small changes and make your thinking visible.

## Core work
1. Write your name, current experience, and the kind of user problem you would like to solve.
2. Choose one observable goal: “I can explain and test a filter” is stronger than “master React.”
3. Describe when you can work and one constraint a reviewer should know.
4. Ask one specific question about the first repository task.

Use this starting structure:

```text
I can already...
By day 7 I want to be able to...
My first project will help...
A constraint I am planning around is...
My question is...
```

## Done checks
- Another person can tell what you will practise and how to recognize improvement.
- Your question includes enough context to answer.
- You have read the next task before choosing an unrealistic commitment.

## If you get stuck
Write what you know and label uncertainty honestly. Do not share passwords, access tokens, phone numbers, or private student information.

## Hand-in and reflection
Put your introduction in the task note or team chat. Ask your reviewer whether the goal can be observed in a real artifact. After feedback, rewrite one vague sentence.

**Optional stretch:** ask a teammate what kind of feedback helps them most. Stop after the timebox; the goal is a shared starting point.
