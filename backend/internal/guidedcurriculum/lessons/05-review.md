# A short weekly update that enables help

**Estimated effort:** 20 minutes. **Prerequisite:** your first week's artifacts. This is a deliberately lighter review and catch-up day.

## Core work
Write a concise update in three sections:
- **Shipped:** link one or two finished artifacts and describe the user-visible result.
- **Blocked:** state one precise blocker, the last experiment you tried and the help you need.
- **Next:** choose one achievable next action and how you will know it is done.

Before posting, reread your learning log. Correct one claim that is now too vague or inaccurate. Close one small review comment if you can; otherwise explain why it remains open.

## Done checks
The update distinguishes finished work from work in progress. A teammate can act on the blocker without guessing the context. The next action fits the available time instead of adding a new feature list.

## Retrieval exercise
Without notes, explain staging versus committing and main axis versus cross axis. Check your explanation afterward and record one correction. Forgetting is useful evidence of what to practise, not a reason to hide.

## Hand-in and feedback
Post the update in your task note or team chat. Ask the reviewer whether the blocker has a clear next action. Use remaining time for feedback and recovery rather than optional stretch work.

**Optional stretch:** ask a teammate for one useful habit they noticed. Do not turn the recovery day into a backlog of extra tasks.
