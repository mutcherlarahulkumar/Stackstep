# Consolidate before adding another framework

**Estimated effort:** 45 minutes. **Prerequisite:** a portfolio PR and review feedback. This is a lighter day for catching up and making knowledge retrievable.

## Core work
1. Spend ten minutes without notes explaining Git staging/committing/pushing, Flexbox axes, and why your two filtering conditions compose with AND.
2. Compare the explanation with your code. Correct one misconception in your task notes.
3. Close one review comment, or make its next action explicit.
4. Plan two observable goals for week two, including a way to check each one.

```text
Goal: I can derive filtered projects from a single dataset.
Evidence: a combined-filter example and an automated test.
Support: ask for a review of my state model before expanding the UI.
```

## Done checks
Your plan includes a realistic timebox and a feedback point. You identify at least one thing you can now explain and one thing you still need to practise. No new feature is necessary to pass this task.

## Debug your learning process
If you keep copying the same solution without being able to explain it, reduce the problem to three items and predict the result before running code. If you are behind, prioritize the hero and filter core over optional stretch work.

## Hand-in and reflection
Share the retrieval correction and next week's two goals in your task note. Ask your reviewer which goal is too broad and rewrite it.

**Optional stretch:** help a teammate improve their reproduction steps. Stop at the timebox and leave space for rest.
